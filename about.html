My Story

My name is Mahider Feleke. I am a Software Engineering student at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (AASTU) in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. I enjoy learning by experimenting, building projects, and working through technical problems.

From assistive technology to data driven applications, I am most engaged when I can turn an idea into something practical, test it, and improve it using evidence and feedback.

My Background

I am pursuing a degree in Software Engineering at AASTU, with an expected graduation in 2029. Alongside university, I have strengthened my algorithmic problem solving through A2SV Generation 7 and gained applied AI/ML project experience through the Gheero residency.
